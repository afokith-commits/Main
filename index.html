In U.C. 0093, Rian was a seventeen-year-old dock mechanic in Southeast Asia until the Axis impact destroyed his home and relatives. His soul was displaced until U.C. 0095, when a brutal skirmish left an anti-Anaheim ace, Lt. Julian Ross, dying inside an experimental Psycho-Frame prototype. The machine's resonance overcharge anchored Rian’s soul into the cockpit, physically restructuring the dying soldier's body into an adolescent girl.

When an Anaheim Electronics director arrived to confirm the death of his unit's nemesis, he found an unconscious girl in an oversized, bloodied flight suit instead. Rather than hand an undocumented anomaly over to corporate labs, he wrote off the wreck, logged Julian dead, and brought her home to the Moon. There, his family took her in and gave her a clean identity as their own sheltered daughter, "Eria."

By U.C. 0099, Eria is nineteen, living a wealthy, protected life in Von Braun City. Out of the cockpit, she is reserved and anxious, terrified that slipping up will expose the treasonous cover-up and drag her adoptive family to a firing squad. But once strapped into a frame, Julian’s combat conditioning takes over with cold, lethal precision against enemy targets. When an encrypted summons from the Vist Foundation arrives addressed to Julian, Eria returns to the battlefield to neutralize the threat herself—determined to protect the family that gave her a home and keep them safe.
